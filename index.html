Questions

Good ones, answered.

Can I try it before I commit?

Yes — grab a $29 day pass. If your first day isn't great, email us and we'll refund it.

Can I bring a guest?

Each membership and pass covers one person. If a friend wants to join you for the day, they can buy a day pass alongside yours.

Do you have parking?

Yes — free street parking on Riverside and the surrounding blocks. Plenty of room except on the busiest summer weekends.

Can I rent the conference room without a membership?

Absolutely. $29 for the first hour, $10 for each additional hour. Reserve it here.

What happens when the power or internet goes out?

Battery backup keeps essential lights and our router running through short outages. The fiber line is the most reliable connection in the valley, but if it does drop, you'll know within seconds — and so will we.
